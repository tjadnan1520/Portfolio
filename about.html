The short version

Engineer by training,
builder by habit

I'm a Software Engineering undergraduate at the Institute of Information Technology, University of Dhaka, holding a CGPA of 3.66 across five completed semesters. I live on campus at Dr. Muhammad Shahidullah Hall.

My work sits where web engineering meets machine learning. I enjoy the full arc of a product — modelling the data, writing the API, tuning the query plan, and then fighting CSS until the thing actually feels good to use. Seven projects later, that range still excites me more than any single layer.

Outside coursework the two results I'm proudest of are competitive: finalisting the SUST CSE Carnival hackathon in the top 15 of 724 teams, and taking runner-up at the 3rd National Science Quiz against competitors from across the country.

I've also organised and evaluated a national scholarship exam for over 1,000 candidates, presented a poster at the 1st S. N. Bose National Science Festival, and led a scout patrol across 10 national and 3 international jamborees.

I'm currently a Software Engineering Intern at KAZ Software (May–Oct 2026), and open to collaborations on anything involving full-stack systems, applied ML, or IoT.

Principles I build by
