About

Engineer by training,
builder by habit

I'm a Software Engineering undergraduate at the Institute of Information Technology, University of Dhaka, maintaining a CGPA of 3.66 through five semesters. My day-to-day work sits at the intersection of web engineering and machine learning — I like the front-end polish and the query that finally makes the query fast.

I've shipped seven projects spanning geospatial routing, a PyTorch small language model, a JavaFX point-of-sale system, algorithm visualisers, game AI, and ESP32-based IoT. Most of them started as coursework and ended up as things I actually use.

Beyond the editor: I finalist-ed the SUST CSE Carnival hackathon with my team, placing in the top 15 of 724 teams, organised and evaluated a national scholarship exam for over 1,000 candidates, and volunteered at ITVerse-2023. I'm currently open for collaboration on interesting projects.
